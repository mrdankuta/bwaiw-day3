# Monthly Budgeting

Personal monthly budgeting for a single user: plan expected money in against categorized money out and explain the result.

## Language

**Inflow**:
Any money received in the month, recurring or not.
_Avoid_: income (when meaning all money in), earnings

**Income**:
The recurring expected portion of inflow (e.g. salary).
_Avoid_: inflow, paycheck (as a generic term)

**Expense**:
Money planned to go out, with a category, amount, and due date.
_Avoid_: spend, cost, bill (as a generic term)

**Fixed Expense**:
An expense with a stable amount each month (e.g. rent).
_Avoid_: recurring expense, standing cost

**Variable Expense**:
An expense whose amount changes month to month (e.g. feeding).
_Avoid_: flexible spend

**One-off Expense**:
A non-recurring expense that occurs once (e.g. appliance repair).
_Avoid_: irregular expense, ad-hoc spend

**Expense Category**:
The bucket an expense belongs to: Housing, Food, Transport, Utilities, Health, Savings, Other.
_Avoid_: tag, label, group

**Plan**:
The deterministic monthly allocation table: inflows minus expenses equals leftover.
_Avoid_: budget (as the artifact), forecast

**Advice**:
Three to five LLM-generated suggestions explaining the plan and trade-offs.
_Avoid_: recommendation engine output, tips (as the formal term)

**Warning**:
A rule breach surfaced from the plan (e.g. rent above threshold, no savings).
_Avoid_: alert, error

**Conservative Income**:
The planning baseline for irregular earners: the lowest or average expected inflow.
_Avoid_: projected income, estimated pay

**Savings Rollover**:
Leftover from a month carried forward as savings into the next month.
_Avoid_: carryover, balance transfer

**Savings Goal**:
A monthly savings target plus an emergency-fund aim expressed in months of essentials.
_Avoid_: savings plan, investment target

**Emergency Fund**:
Saved money measured in months of essential expenses.
_Avoid_: rainy-day fund (as the formal term), reserves

**Debt Payment**:
A debt minimum treated as an expense in the plan.
_Avoid_: loan installment (as the generic term), liability
